Our Inspection Process

Every sofa passes 8 checks before it reaches your door

Buying preloved shouldn't mean rolling the dice. We know exactly what people worry about — bed bugs, odours, hidden damage — so we built our whole process around ruling those out. If a sofa doesn't pass every stage, it doesn't get listed. No exceptions.

We turn away far more sofas than we accept. That's the point.

🛡️
Our Bed Bug & Pest Protocol

Bed bugs are the number one reason people avoid used furniture in Toronto — and they're right to ask. Here's exactly what we do about it.

Seam-by-seam inspection. Bed bugs hide in seams, piping, staple lines and frame joints — not out in the open. We strip every cushion, unzip every cover and inspect every crevice under bright light before a sofa ever leaves the pickup address.

We walk away from anything questionable. If a home shows any sign of an active infestation, we don't take the sofa — even if it looks spotless. It isn't worth the risk to you or to us.

Quarantine before listing. Accepted pieces are sealed, treated and held separately in our storage facility before they're photographed and listed.

8
Point inspection on every sofa
01
🏠
Source Vetting
We ask about the home before we ever load a sofa — pets, smoking, how long they owned it, why it's going. Vague answers mean we pass.
02
🔦
Seam & Crevice Check
Cushions off, covers unzipped, bright light on every seam, staple line and frame joint — the places pests actually hide.
03
🪑
Frame & Structure
We sit on it, lean on it, lift it. Wobbly frames, broken springs, sagging decks and cracked joints are automatic rejections.
04
👃
Odour Test
Smoke and pet odour sink into foam and rarely come out. If we can smell it, we don't take it — a cover-up scent isn't a fix.
05
🧼
Deep Clean & Extraction
Every accepted sofa gets a full clean — upholstery treated, cushions cleaned inside and out, crevices vacuumed thoroughly.
06
🔒
Sealed Quarantine
Wrapped and held separately in our storage facility before listing — never mixed straight in with ready stock.
07
📸
Honest Photography
Real photos in our storage unit, natural light, no filters. Any mark or wear gets photographed and written into the listing.
08
✅
Final Pre-Delivery Check
One last inspection on the morning of delivery. If anything has changed since listing, we tell you before we load it.
🤝
See something we missed? Tell us at the door.
Inspect your sofa when we arrive, before you pay the balance. If it doesn't match the listing or the photos, don't take it — we'll load it back up and refund your $49 deposit in full. No arguments, no restocking fee, no awkwardness.
